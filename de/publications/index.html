Visual Cue Guided Video Planning for Generalizable Robot Navigation

Hojin Lee, Sizhe Lester Li, Maximilian Hilger, Susie Lu, Achim J. Lilienthal, Vincent Sitzmann, Daniel-André Dücker

arXivProjekt
Zusammenfassung

People drive through places they have never seen by reading visual cues such as a map, a landmark, or the shape of the road ahead. CueNav gives a robot the same ability: a video planner imagines the drive, flow fields turn the imagined video into motion, and an inverse dynamics model turns motion into commands for the specific robot, replanning as it goes.

TreeBotic: Learning-Based Robotics as an Enabler for Biodiversity-Aware Agroforestry Systems

Lennart Trösken

Workshop-Beitrag
Zusammenfassung

TreeBotic proposes a framework for robotics in agroforestry, where trees and crops share a changing environment. It combines learning from human demonstrations and corrections, environmental indicators that inform robot decisions, and coupled robotic and ecological simulation. These components are intended to help evaluate field-management strategies with biodiversity as an explicit objective.
